Sign the Guestbook

Leave a note about the site. Tell us which experiment worked for you, which one did not, or just say hi. Entries are stored in your browser and shown on the wall below.

This guestbook is deliberately old-fashioned. There is no account to make, no server to send anything to, and no analytics watching you type. When you post, the entry is written to your browser's own storage and rendered onto the wall underneath the form. Clear your browser data and it is gone, because your browser was the only copy. That also means the wall you see is yours: another visitor on another machine sees their own. The email field is required by the form, and it is deliberately never stored, not even locally.
